Developed at Digital University Kerala, it lets you define a bounding box and date range, browse the matching scenes with their acquisition date and cloud-cover metadata, and stream a selected scene to disk in the background without blocking the QGIS interface. An optional DLR EOWEB bearer token can be supplied for restricted assets.
